# Lufthansa Surprise Flight Optimizer & Risk Engine (v0.1 Prototype)

Research Prototype for optimizing Lufthansa Surprise bookings under strict personal schedule and risk constraints.

## 1. Actual Use Case & Functionality
Lufthansa Surprise offers cheap mystery flights from FRA to various European destinations. Users can deselect destinations they do not want to visit, but each deselected checkbox increases the fare. Once booked, Lufthansa automatically allocates one of the active destinations and a flight itinerary, which cannot be changed or cancelled.

**The Problem:** The allocated flight times might be highly unfavorable (e.g. departing too early on Friday, or returning too early on Sunday), violating the user's personal travel window (e.g. "outbound flight must start at or after 14:00 on Friday").

**The Solution:** This optimizer scrapes flight schedules, maps pool checkbox pricing dependencies, calculates itinerary combinations, filters them using overnight-dependent stay constraints, and recommends whether to KEEP a pool, REDUCE it to flight-compatible options, or SKIP booking entirely.

## 2. Empirical Pricing Findings & Platform Rules
Based on live experiments on the Lufthansa Surprise platform, the following findings are verified:

* **Minimum Active Targets:** The UI requires **at least 3 active targets** to be checked. De-selecting more than 8 targets is blocked by the platform.
* **Base Prices:** 
  * The base price of **109.00 EUR** was historically observed for cheaper surprise pools (e.g., "Nature & Outdoor") or during low-demand booking windows.
  * The base price of **129.00 EUR** is the active baseline for the "Arts & Culture" (Kunst und Kultur) pool under current seasonal conditions.
* **Leave-one-out and Pool Pricing Points:**
  * **11 active targets:** 129.00 EUR (Baseline)
  * **10 active targets, observed case:** 138.58 EUR (Leave-one-out with a standard target deactivated)
  * **10 active targets without LIN:** 140.32 EUR (Leave-one-out with Milan deactivated)
  * **9 active targets (FLR, PRG inactive):** 144.56 EUR ($k=2$ repair validation run)
  * **8 active targets (FLR, PRG, WAW inactive):** 149.67 EUR ($k=3$ repair validation run)
  * **3 active targets (FLR, PRG, WAW):** 177.52 EUR
  * **3 active targets (HEL, BLQ, KRK):** 204.89 EUR

* **Pricing Premium Rule:**
  * Bei bisher beobachteten Leave-one-out-Konfigurationen entstand ein Aufschlag von überwiegend 9,58 EUR, mit mindestens einem abweichenden Wert (LIN de-selection).
  * Bei Drei-Ziele-Konfigurationen ist der Preis deutlich zusammensetzungsabhängig (z.B. 177,52 EUR vs. 204,89 EUR).
  * Der Übergang zwischen großen und kleinen Pools ist noch ungemessen.
  * Linear extrapolations are **not** verified rules and must not be used to predict prices for unmeasured configurations.

## 3. Strict Separation of Concerns & Epistemics
The engine strictly separates different layers of analysis:
1. **Observed Configuration Prices:** What the platform charges for a given checkbox selection (Baseline vs. Flex-Malus).
2. **Assumed Target Availability:** The actual inventory availability (which cities have active flight options).
3. **Public Flight Schedules:** Retrieved direct flights and scheduled timings (from SerpApi or public schedules).
4. **Personal Risk Rules:** Constraints like "earliest outbound >= 14:00" and overnight-dependent stay rules.
5. **Final Recommendation:** The integrated decision (KEEP_POOL, REDUCE_POOL, SKIP) generated by the decision engine.

### Epistemic Status Fields
Decisions are evaluated using separate parameters to avoid assuming availability from compatibility:
* `schedule_compatibility`: Safe/mixed/unsafe (refers ONLY to flight plan compatibility, not booking safety).
* `observed_pool_availability`: Current platform response availability.
* `target_inventory_status`: Whether Lufthansa Surprise actually holds seat inventory (evaluated as `unknown` unless confirmed).
* `allocation_probability`: Probability of allocating a target (`unknown` without internal platform seat data).
* `recommendation`: Final action.

#### The Current Pula (PUY) Case
For the current case, the epistemic status is:
* `schedule_compatibility` = safe (the flight schedule matches the Friday >= 14:00 constraint)
* `target_inventory_status` = unknown (we cannot verify seat availability)
* `allocation_probability` = unknown (allocation probabilities are unknown)
* `recommendation` = **SKIP** (the pool cannot be safely booked or reduced to PUY due to the 3-target checkbox limit. We do **not** claim PUY is available or allocatable).

## 4. Risk Representation & Confidence
* **No Implicit Probabilities:** We do not derive statistical allocation probabilities from categories like `safe`, `mixed`, or `unsafe`.
* **Output Metrics:**
  * Number of safely suitable targets (compatible flight times)
  * Number of mixed targets
  * Number of untested targets
  * Allocation distribution: `unknown`
  * Confidence: `low`, `medium`, or `high` with an explicit reason (e.g. `low` because the configuration limit prevents meeting schedule constraints).

---

## 5. Overnight-Dependent Stay Constraints
* **1 Night Stay:** Minimum stay of **18 hours** between outbound arrival and return departure.
* **2 Nights Stay:** Minimum stay of **36 hours** between outbound arrival and return departure.

---

## 6. Playwright Test Stability (Control Groups)
To guarantee data stability and prevent caching/session interference:
* A baseline search with all 11 active targets is executed **before** and **after** every test run.
* If the baseline prices before and after match (129.00 EUR), the intermediate test values are verified as stable measurements.

---

## 7. Regular Price Comparison Schema
For future comparison calls, regular flight options should be evaluated against Lufthansa Surprise using the following schema:
* **Identical Travel Dates:** Compare exact matching calendar days.
* **Departure Windows:** Only compare flight options departing after 14:00.
* **Direct Flight Preference:** Direct flights preferred.
* **Baggage and Tariff Conditions:** Document baggage limits (e.g., personal item only vs. carry-on) and cancellation rules.
* **Surprise Price vs. Regular Price:** Compute the absolute difference.
* **Control Value:** Separately calculate the economic value of keeping target selection control and time control.
